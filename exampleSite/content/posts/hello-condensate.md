This is the first sample post. Condensate keeps the page almost entirely type: a slab-serif title, a condensed date line, and a comfortable serif for reading.

<!--more-->

Everything below the summary marker only appears on the post page. Links look [like this](https://gohugo.io/), and `inline code` gets a subtle background.

## Headings and lists

Second-level headings use the slab face.

- Plain bulleted lists
- With sensible spacing
- And no decoration

> Blockquotes are set in italics and indented.
